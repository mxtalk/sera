## Method and findings

Every one of the 422 fires was judged in its sentence against the three measures. The text is a mature Wikipedia article, and almost every use already does what the measures ask: definite articles sit on referents the surrounding words single out (names, superlatives, of-phrases, back-references), and joins things in sum or in a sequence the wording itself names, and of marks belonging (part, member, attribute, content) nearly everywhere.

Two fires were repaired, both the same failure: an idiom where of marks something other than belonging.

- p3.of2 — "by means of" uses of for instrument. Repair names the instrument relation plainly: "using either geodetic coordinates …".
- p22.of1 — "in terms of" uses of for the respect in which locations are described. Repair: "are described by geodetic latitude …".

Fires inside proper names, quoted definitions (the NAD 27 and NAD 83 definitions are direct quotations), and cited titles are recorded clear with reasoning beginning "fixed wording", per the survey's rule, and were not repaired. Citation boilerplate ("Archived from the original on …") was judged on its merits: each citation resolves its own original.

No list errors were found: all 422 fires in fires.csv were located in text.md, in the pieces and order given, and the sheet's fire/fix/original columns are handed back byte-identical.
