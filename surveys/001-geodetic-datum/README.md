## text

text.md holds everything that is the article, as a desktop reader receives it, in page order. Each piece sits on its own line, numbered from p1.

Left out: hatnotes ("Main article: …" and the like), the Geodesy sidebar, the portal box, the Wiktionary box, the short description, categories, language links, edit links, the ↑ back-links in references, hidden maintenance messages, and site menus and notices.

Kept as readers see them: footnote markers such as [1] and [note 1], [citation needed] tags, and the numbered external link [1] in the Examples list.

Changed:

- Maths is the TeX from the wikitext's `<math>` tags, between `$…$`.
- Non-breaking spaces are plain spaces, and repeated white space is one space.
- Bold and italics are dropped; captions are in *italics*.
- Markdown marks show each piece's form: `# p1` title, `## p13` and `### p14` headings, `p2 *…*` captions, `| p25 … |` table rows (cells joined by " | ", a separator line after each table's first row), `- p59` bullets, `1. p100` ordered items keeping their own numbers.

## fire list

fires.csv has a row for each *the*, *and* and *of* in text.md, as a whole word, outside `$…$` maths: 422 fires, *the* 227, *of* 107, *and* 88.

**original** is the sentence holding the fire. These pieces stand whole: the title, headings, table rows, table captions, and the items under References and Further reading.

Elsewhere, a sentence ends at . ! ? or …, after any closing quote, bracket or footnote marker, where a space and then a capital letter follow, with or without an opening quote or bracket. The split skips these abbreviations: U.S., e.g., i.e., etc., vs., ca., No., Dr., Mr., Mrs., St., Inc., pp., p., ed. A bare "..." between sentences joins the sentence before it (p52).
